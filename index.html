FAQ

Questions people ask.

How much does a website cost?

Simple business websites start from ₹5,000. The final price depends on how many pages and features you need. I'll tell you the price before I start.

How long will it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly I get your photos and details. I'll give you a date before we start.

Do I need hosting and a domain?

Yes, a website needs both. They are usually paid separately, and I can explain the options and help set them up.

Can customers message me on WhatsApp from the site?

Yes. Buttons and the enquiry form open WhatsApp with a ready message to your number.

Can you change the website later?

Yes. Message me and we can agree on the change and the cost, if any.

I'm outside Cooch Behar. Can we still work together?

Yes. Everything can be done over WhatsApp and calls.

Do you edit Instagram reels?

Yes. You send the footage and I edit it into vertical 9:16 videos with captions and music.

Can I start with a very simple website?

Yes. Many businesses only need one page at first. We can add more later.
